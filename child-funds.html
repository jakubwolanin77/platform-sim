Should you create a child fund?

If you have children, time is your greatest asset. Investing just €50 a month from birth can result in over €20,000 by their 18th birthday thanks to compound interest.

How to set it up

1. Choose Account

Decide between a custodial account (belongs to child) or investing in your own name earmarked for them.

2. Select Index Fund

Pick a low-cost global index fund. Time horizon is 10+ years, so 100% equity is standard.

3. Automate

Set up an automatic monthly transfer on payday. Set it and forget it.
