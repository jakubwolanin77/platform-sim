How to refinance

  1. Request a payoff quote from your current lender.
  2. Apply for a mortgage with a new lender showing your target rate.
  3. Pay attention to refinancing fees (usually 1-2% of loan balance).

Adding a little extra to your monthly payment goes directly to the principal, drastically reducing total interest.

How to increase payment

  1. Log in to your bank's portal.
  2. Go to Loan details > Repayment schedule.
  3. Select "Make extra payment" or "Change monthly amount".
  4. Ensure the extra amount is applied to principal, not future interest.
